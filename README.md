# Credit Risk & Delinquency Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer delinquency trends, credit risk segmentation, loan exposure, and regional risk distribution for a fintech collections and risk management use case.

The dashboard helps identify high-risk customer segments, monitor delinquency patterns, and support business decision-making through data-driven insights.

---

# Business Problem

Financial institutions and fintech companies need effective ways to monitor customer delinquency and identify risky accounts before defaults increase.

This dashboard was developed to:
- Analyze delinquency behavior
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Monitor loan exposure
- Support collections strategy
- Improve credit risk visibility

# Key Business Insights

- High-risk customers showed elevated loan exposure levels.
- Delinquency rates varied significantly across income groups.
- Geographic regions displayed different delinquency concentrations.
- Certain credit card types contributed more to delinquent accounts.
- Customer credit score patterns revealed potential risk clusters.
